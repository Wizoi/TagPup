| B-1 | 2026-11-03 | low: TagPup's ExifTool IPTC writes leave the Photoshop IPTCDigest (8BIM 0x0425) stale. On copies of 8 kr-track JPEGs (4 whose digest matched, 4 whose digest differed; all last saved by Lightroom Classic), `-IPTC:Keywords+=` changed the IPTC block and its MD5 (ExifTool's CurrentIPTCDigest) but left 0x0425 as it was, so a digest that matched no longer did. The census's comparison is right: its MD5 of 0x0404 equalled ExifTool's CurrentIPTCDigest on all 8. Counted this session (census, read-only): the digest differs in 112 of 3,000 sampled photo_index JPEGs, 1,902 of 2,065 kr-track and 839 of 1,308 renton_parkrun; it matches in 2,093, 153 and 463.
