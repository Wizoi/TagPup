## 4. The three most expensive episodes

1. **A library on a new model broke every reader that assumed FaceNet.** 17 `wrong` rows in the phase, 10 of them of
   this kind (#1663, #1667, #1673 on 10-18; #1707, #1710, #1715, #1717, #1719, #1721, #1728 on 10-20), most in the last
   merge, which added 1,653 lines. Earliest signal: #1667 and #1673 on 10-18, when "a switched library" was a new
   state. Cheaper path: the brief lists every reader of vectors and cuts as its scenarios (the sweep CLAUDE.md asks
   for). Partly the cost of learning.
2. **The model was read in separate statements from the vectors it keys.** #1656, #1662 (decided by design), then
   #1709, #1722-#1726, fixed by one owner (`FaceRows` carrying its key, e82882c4). Earliest signal: #1662, the second
   row of the symptom; by CLAUDE.md the second fix for one symptom is the ownership question. Cheaper path: ask it
   there, when the key could already have travelled with the vectors. Known then.
3. **31 of 40 new faces were sub-40 px smudges.** Found by the owner looking at a real folder, not by the dry run,
   which reports counts. Earliest signal: the old detector's size distribution (smallest 23 px, median 64) and the
   named trade-off about small faces (DECISIONS 2026-10-16). Cheaper path: the dry run shows sizes beside the old
   model's. Cost: not counted.

Not episodes from the record: the two live-library writes the auto-mode classifier blocked until the owner said so (a
one-way choice on live data is the owner's: the guard did its job; no action), and the 150k hook firing on most worker
tasks (hook firings are not counted by the script).

## 8. What to carry to other projects

- A second finding of one symptom is the signal to ask who owns the rule; a count of such rows per class is the
  metric.
- A dry run for a model swap must show the output, not only its counts: the owner looking at one real sample caught what
  counts could not.
- An exception to a budget (`--allow-over`) should leave its reason where the metrics read it.
- A retro's data file must be checked against the ledger it counts: a qualifier after a severity word hid 7 `wrong`
  rows.
