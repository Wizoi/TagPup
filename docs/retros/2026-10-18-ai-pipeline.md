## 4. The three most expensive episodes

**E1. Worker tokens tied to no merge (cost, not countable).** 226.3 M tokens (160..463 USD) sit in 20 branches that
the script cannot tie to a merge, because a worker's work was merged under its own branch name (`perf/...`) while the
run is named by its worktree. The largest run used 70 M tokens in 223 calls (about 314k a call, twice the budget). The
earliest signal was the last retro's own caveat that the figure was a range until a sample was read. Cheaper path:
record the worktree in the merge, so the join is made once, at merge. Owner of the rule: `retro_metrics.py --branch`.

**E2. "Nothing blocks the merge" with 11 findings open (escape to the owner).** The owner found 8 phase-0 lows and
3 more open; 1b waited; its reviews then opened 19 lows. Two instructions were both in context: CLAUDE.md ("only data
and wrong block a merge") and DECISIONS 2026-10-15 ("a low is fixed or by design, no third state"). The report followed
the first and the owner expected the second. Cheaper path: the open count from the file in every report. Owner of the
rule: `tools/add_findings.py`.

**E3. A decision resting on a premise nobody checked (rework).** DECISIONS 2026-10-15 said the indexer runs
cluster-faces after every index; no entry point did (#1544), and the library had no run since 2026-09-23. The chain
cost about 18 ledger rows and two decisions, and the first live run exposed a defect that was there all along (#1582).
No action: the cost of learning (the premise was incidental to the row that recorded it; the owner of People counted it
within a day).

## 8. What to carry to other projects

- A rule that lives only in memory is broken the first time the count is not in front of the reader: give the count a
  command and make reports quote it.
- If a metric joins two records by name, record the name at the moment of the merge, not afterwards.
- A hook's limit for a subagent needs the subagent's own transcript, not the session's.
- The drafting agent found two script weaknesses (an unclosed `planned:` row counted open; the first-parent window
  start including the day before): the script is the place to fix them, not the data file.
